  STEP 3 — MLController.java (complete, 225 lines above)

  Key observations from the full source:

  DSO1 /student/{studentId}/conversion (lines 45–97):
  - Pulls real DB data: enrollmentRepository.findByStudentId(),
  lessonProgressRepository.findByEnrollmentId()
  - Computes: completionRate (avg of enrollment percentages / 100), timeSpent
  (sum of timeSpentSeconds / 60), videosWatched (count of COMPLETED lesson
  progress), paidEnrollments (count with non-blank paymentIntentId)
  - Passes to Flask: timeSpent, completionRate, 50.0 (hardcoded quizScores), 
  videosWatched, enrollments.size(), 0.0 (hardcoded forumReads)
  - quizScores is hardcoded to 50.0 and forumReads is hardcoded to 0.0 — two of
  six DSO1 features are placeholder constants

  DSO3 quality (lines 101–216):
